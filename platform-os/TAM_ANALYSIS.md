# TAM & Revenue Analysis — Academy OS (UK youth football)

*Compiled 18 June 2026. Figures sourced; estimates and assumptions flagged explicitly.*

## Sourced market data

- ~**18,000** FA-accredited grassroots clubs in England (current FA figure).
- ~**119,000** teams in affiliated football, ~**1.8 million** players, **1,200+** leagues — FA, but a **2015** figure covering **all ages incl. adults**.
- **40%** of children in England played football in 2023–24 (Statista); ~**11 million** play football in England overall.
- FA 2024–28 strategy: **>50% of affiliated football expected in 20+ team clubs by 2028** — multi-team clubs (our buyer) are the growth segment.
- FORZA 2025 grassroots survey: **94%** of clubs run on unpaid volunteers; **72%** lack adequate funding; subscriptions (90%) are the main revenue — relevant to willingness/ability to pay.

## TAM build — youth U7–U16, UK

The FA does **not** publish a clean U7–U16 team count, so this is an explicit estimate:

| Step | Figure | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliated teams, England, all ages | 119,000 | FA (2015) |
| Youth U7–U16 share | ~50% → ~60,000 | **Assumption** (youth/mini-soccer is the largest segment; not an FA-published split) |
| UK uplift (Scotland/Wales/NI, +~15%) | **~70,000** | England ≈ 84% of UK football |
| Sensitivity range | 55,000 – 85,000 | conservative ↔ optimistic |

## Revenue at £9.99/team/month (= £119.88/team/year)

| Scenario | TAM (teams) | 10% capture | Annual revenue at 10% |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 55,000 | 5,500 | £659,340 |
| **Central** | **70,000** | **7,000** | **£839,160** |
| Optimistic | 85,000 | 8,500 | £1,018,980 |

- 100% of central TAM ≈ **£8.39M/year** (total addressable revenue).
- If priced across *all* affiliated teams (119,000, all ages), 10% ≈ **£1.43M/year**.

## Competitors

15+ notable tools: Spond, TeamSnap, Heja, Pitchero, Veo, Playmaker, FA Matchday, TeamStats, Coacha, 360Player, FirstWhistle, TopTekkers, Coach Logic, Trace, PlayMetrics. Per `COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md`, the market is crowded on **admin/logistics** and largely empty on **integrated player development + admin + club-down white-label** — the identified white space (time-boxed; FirstWhistle/360Player closing in).

## Honest caveats (these concern *capture*, not market size)

1. **Free incumbents.** Spond and FA Matchday are free to clubs. The TAM is real; the genuine unknown is converting **10% at £9.99/team** against free — which only building/selling can answer.
2. **Dated/estimated inputs.** The 119,000 is 2015; the youth share is our assumption, not FA-published. Treat the central case as order-of-magnitude, not precise.
3. **Per-team vs per-club pricing.** £9.99 × a 104-team club = ~£1,038/month; real pricing will likely band by club size — which raises revenue per club rather than shrinking TAM.
