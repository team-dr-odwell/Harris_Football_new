# Feature Verification — Admin-Leaning Competitors vs Academy OS

**Date:** 18 June 2026
**Method:** Web search against each product's own marketing site / help docs where possible. Every row carries a one-line evidence note + source URL. Anything not confirmable from a primary or credible source is marked **TBD** (not assumed present).
**Scope:** Strict apples-to-apples against the Academy OS shipped feature set (Admin/Logistics, Player Development, Family Engagement, Club/Platform).

> **Legend:** Yes = clearly present · Partial = related but narrower than ours · No = credibly absent · TBD = could not verify from a trustworthy source.

---

## Headline finding

**None of the six admin-leaning competitors ship structured player development** in the Academy OS sense — i.e. a points/rewards engine with earning rules, FA four-corners learning/quizzes, a skill ladder with coach sign-off, individual development plans, or video reflections that award development %. They are scheduling / communications / payments / club-admin tools. The closest any of them gets is **appearance/match-stats tracking** (Pitchero, TeamStats, FA Matchday) and generic "monitor player progress" marketing language (Spond) — none of which is a development curriculum or a gamified learning loop.

Where each clearly **beats Academy OS**: in-app messaging/chat (all except FA Matchday is web-only on our side), payment/subscription collection (Spond, Pitchero, Coacha, TeamStats), native iOS/Android apps (all except our web-only app), and club websites/CMS + online shop (Pitchero).
