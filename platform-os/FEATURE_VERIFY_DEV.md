# Feature Verification — Development-Leaning Competitors

**Date:** 18 June 2026
**Method:** Web verification against each product's own marketing site / help docs / app-store listings. Every claim is cited. Anything not verifiable from a primary source is marked **TBD** — not assumed.
**Reference product:** Academy OS (our shipped feature set, listed below).
**Competitors verified:** 360Player, TopTekkers, TeamSnap (incl. TeamSnap ONE), FirstWhistle, Coach Logic.

Scoring key: **Yes** = clearly present per source · **Partial** = related capability but not the same depth/mechanic · **No** = clearly absent or out of scope · **TBD** = could not verify from a primary source.

---

## Dimension reference (Academy OS)

**A. Admin/Logistics:** fixtures & results; training-session scheduling with drills attached; events; availability/RSVP; one-tap attendance register + coach "who's coming" view; squad/roster management; computed league table & season stats; multi-team club layer; per-family logins with coach/parent/player roles; multi-child family accounts.

**B. Player development (our core):** evolving/tiered player cards; Academy Points engine with earning rules; weekly quizzes mapped to FA four corners; weekly challenges; skill ladder with coach sign-off; curated video library (tagged, per-child assignment); video reflections (watch → comment → submit → auto-award development %); individual development plans / position targets; development progress tracking (%); badges / Mover of the Month / achievements; squad-wide goals; leaderboards/gamification.

**C. Family engagement:** parent hub to confirm homework; "home team" chores / personal goals; homework gate; parent-VISIBLE player development (not just logistics).

**D. Club/platform:** club-down white-label (subdomain per club); club-level rollup oversight; GDPR-restricted sponsor pages/logins.

## Verdict — who actually matches our DEVELOPMENT + ADMIN combination

**No competitor matches Academy OS on the full development core.** Our distinctive mechanics — a configurable Academy Points engine with earning rules, FA four-corners weekly **quiz bank**, video reflections that **auto-award a development %**, a skill ladder with **coach sign-off**, a homework gate, and the parent "Home Team" chores layer — are not present in any of the five. Each competitor lands in one of three camps:

- **Admin-only (development is logistics/content, not pedagogy):** **TeamSnap** — by far the strongest admin/logistics + payments + registration + tournaments, but essentially zero individual-development engine. **FirstWhistle** sits here too (admin + stats + AI tactics + a light "achievements" layer), with more dev-flavour ambition but no structured curriculum.

- **Development-only (little/no team admin):** **TopTekkers** — strong individual skill development with challenges, trophies, progress charts and **global leaderboards** (the only competitor with genuine gamification + leaderboards), plus coach learning plans and a parent portal — but no fixtures, scheduling, attendance, or league tables. **Coach Logic** — pure collaborative video analysis with genuine player video reflection, but no admin and no progress/points mechanics.

- **Development + admin hybrid (closest in shape):** **360Player** — the only product that combines real club admin (scheduling, roster, registration, payments, comms, website) with a real individual-development product (assessments → auto-generated IDPs, position-matching, goals, performance reviews, video, stats). It is the **single closest like-for-like product to Academy OS in overall scope.**

**Single closest like-for-like:** **360Player.** It matches the breadth (admin + development + club layer) better than anyone. **However, its development is assessment/review/algorithm-driven, not gamified** — it has no points engine, no four-corners quiz bank, no challenges, no badges/leaderboards, no homework gate, and no video-reflection-auto-award. So even the closest competitor does **not** replicate our gamified, child-facing, parent-gated development engine.

**Where we are uniquely differentiated (no competitor verified to have these):** the rule-based Academy Points engine; weekly FA four-corners quizzes; video reflections that auto-award development %; skill ladder with coach sign-off; homework gate; Home Team chores/personal-goals layer; GDPR-restricted sponsor pages.

**Where the field is ahead of us (common gaps to note):** native iOS/Android apps (all five except, web-only like us, none — 360Player, TopTekkers, TeamSnap, FirstWhistle, Coach Logic all ship native apps); in-app messaging/chat (360Player, TeamSnap, FirstWhistle); integrated payments/registration (360Player, TeamSnap, FirstWhistle); and gamified global leaderboards (TopTekkers).
