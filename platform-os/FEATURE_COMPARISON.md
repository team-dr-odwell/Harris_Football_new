## 5. Verdict

**Do competitors offer the same service? No — but the honest picture is two-sided.**

- **On admin/logistics, we are at parity at best, and behind on delivery.** Several products match our scheduling/RSVP/attendance/league features, and they beat us on things we don't have at all: native apps (every competitor), in-app chat (most), payment/subs collection (Spond, TeamSnap, Pitchero, Coacha, 360Player, TeamStats), and full club websites/shops (Pitchero). On commodity admin we are not differentiated and are missing table-stakes (a native app, payments).

- **On player development, nobody matches our combination.** The signature mechanics — a rule-based points engine, FA four-corners quiz bank, challenges, a skill ladder with coach sign-off, video reflections that auto-award development %, the homework gate, and Home-Team chores — are absent across all competitors verified.

- **The closest like-for-like is 360Player** — the only product that genuinely combines club admin with an individual-development product (skill assessments → auto-generated IDPs, PositionMatch, reviews, video). But its development is **assessment/algorithm-driven, not gamified**: no points, quizzes, challenges, badges, leaderboards, homework gate, or auto-award reflections. It also beats us today on native apps, chat and payments.
- **TopTekkers** is closest on *gamified development* but does no team admin; **Coach Logic** has the nearest video-reflection analogue but no auto-award and no admin.

**Conclusion:** the "integrated gamified academy + team admin + club white-label" combination is genuinely unoccupied — that is the real, defensible apples-to-apples differentiation. But to be compared apples-to-apples *and win*, we must reach parity on the table stakes we currently lack (native app experience, payments, in-app comms), or we will be judged "less complete" on the admin half regardless of our development edge.
